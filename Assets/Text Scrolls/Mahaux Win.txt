As soon as the 7th cycle ended, I heard a commotion within the temple walls. Before I could round the final corner to the main hall, I witnessed one of the acolytes dragging a bloodied peer by his hair. Without acknowledging me she began smashing the wounded disciple’s head into the stone wall, each impact accompanied by a guttural scream. She ended her assault well after the fallen acolyte had gone limp and well after his face had been reduced to a formless crimson pulp.

She dropped his body and wordlessly returned to the main hall. I followed and immediately had to shield my eyes; the eastern wall of the temple had been destroyed, allowing a bloodthirsty, febrile sunrise to flood the once peaceful hall. The rays illuminated a flowing river of blood, entrails, and severed limbs, each appearing to have been torn asunder by rabid human hands. The woman turned and looked at me for the last time, revealing her opaque, blood-red sclera before collapsing in a heap amongst the putrid viscera. 

I alone was spared. I alone would witness the Ascension of Mahuax, God of War, razing the earth by the very hands that once cultivated it.
