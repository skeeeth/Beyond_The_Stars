The 7th cycle came to an end. I knew I had not done enough to ascend any of The Oldest One’s children. As the guilt welled up within me, I felt the ancient ringing rattle my skull. I could not understand the words it was speaking, but I knew that its intent was to punish me. The ringing intensified and I felt all of my gravity pull towards the center of my chest as I rose kilometers into the dawnlit sky. 

I saw mountains collapse, storms churn, and volcanoes erupt. I could hear every casualty from the disasters with deafening clarity. I wanted to close my eyes, but I couldn’t. My eyelids had been seized open. The thin air stung my eyes and my lungs. I could barely breathe, let alone scream. Tears streamed from my eyes until my tear ducts swelled shut. 

I wished it would let me die, but The Oldest One sustained me for months to see the Earth tear itself apart until the only screams I could hear were my own. When I at last heard silence, I knew it was time for my final humiliation. My skin, hair, muscles, and tendons, once hanging on by a thread, began to snap off my body one by one, each more painful than the last. In any other circumstance, I would have died, but the power held by The Oldest One superseded that of earthly physics and biology. The last two fibers of my being took the longest to pull. And then there was nothing.
