As soon as the 7th cycle ended, I felt a profound rumbling beneath the stones of our hidden temple. The acolytes and I stumbled outside to a duskbroken sky. A sweet, intoxicating odor of elderberry and Callery pear swept through the forest, readily inhaled by the yearning acolytes. They fell to the ground and began to writhe and moan in an indecipherable chorus of pain and ecstasy. Their echoes crescendoed into an unbearable ringing that made my head feel as if it were about to split in two, until they stopped altogether. 

In the silence, I lifted my eyes up to the horizon and beheld a majestic sunrise colored with hues of nightshade blooms and tulips. I do not know how long I stared deeply into the blaze, but when I blinked and awoke, I found myself truly alone. The acolytes around me had already begun to decay, their bloodshot eyes and ravenous mouths agape, their skin ruptured with fungus and blood-red tendrils of ivy. 

I alone was spared. I alone would witness the Ascension of Kolma, Goddess of Beauty and Fertility, ravaging a newly fecund earth.
