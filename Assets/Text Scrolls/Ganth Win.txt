As soon as the 7th cycle ended, a thick rain began to fall upon the roof of the temple, each drop like a heavy thud. An acolyte pointed at the ceiling, calling my attention to the bowing support beams of the temple. In an instant, one of the beams splintered and snapped, collapsing a portion of the ceiling and crushing the unfortunate acolytes below. 

A lifeless, gray light filled the temple and shone upon the rubble. Atop the debris was a hill of dead crows, doves, and insects. A desperate acolyte rushed to search for survivors beneath the rubble, but upon stepping into the pallid light he withered and stumbled dead, headfirst into the heap. The building shook and creaked as the roof prepared to fall. 

I ran to exit the building along with the others. As I heard the temple collapse behind me, I looked back to see how many had escaped. All I saw was a pile of corpses blocking the entryway, each frozen in decay and posed with desperate, outstretched arms.

I alone was spared. I alone would witness the Ascension of Gan’th, God of Death, littering the earth with cadavers of every form.
