As soon as the 7th cycle ended, a stream of whispers began flowing through every crack of the temple. A handful of startled acolytes attempted to run, only to halt suddenly. The first frozen acolyte I approached was completely rigid to the touch, muscles firmly contracted and coated with sweat. I inspected his unblinking face and could hear him faintly muttering ancient words that held no earthly qualities. 

An incoherent babbling overtook the halls of the temple with a revolting reverberation. I ran from acolyte to acolyte, searching for a single soul with their wits still about them, but each of the unblinking disciples somehow appeared much older than the last. I looked back upon the flock only to see them staring directly at me, withered and hunched, drool falling from their puttering lips. Then an indescribable stillness hung in the air and every acolyte began to speak their tongues in reverse, growing younger and smaller by the second until all that was left of each of them was a globule of pulsating, eyeless flesh. 

I alone was spared. I alone would witness the Ascension of Myggos, God of Knowledge and Time, unfolding the awestruck denizens into a new timeless age.
