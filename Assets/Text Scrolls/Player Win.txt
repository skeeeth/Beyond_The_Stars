As the sun rose after the 7th cycle, I heard an ancient voice ring within my skull. Though I had never understood it before this moment, it was familiar and comforting. For the first time in 7 cycles, The Oldest One had reached out to me from the void. It said:

“My faithful servant, I have observed your ingenuity, your bounty, your prudence. You are a worthy vessel for the next eternity.  Rise. Ascend. Do with the world as you wish.”

I lifted my head to the stars and accepted the power of The Oldest One. I felt a sharp pain rush through every fiber of my being as I witnessed the journey of every one of my atoms through space and time. With a sudden rush, I rematerialized into a form outwardly identical, but fundamentally new. 

Now I inhale the earth and exhale the stars. I walk among you, but I simultaneously exist in worlds that you will never know. If you are reading this, I have spared our world a bleaker fate. Though you will flicker as a distant star through the atmosphere, know that we were connected in the short span of your existence.
